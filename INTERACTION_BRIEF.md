# Research discovery and interactive explanation

## Scope
Extend the established academic visual identity across Home, Research, Publications, Writing, and three research detail pages. The user approved this architecture and selected interactive experiments as the personal signature. Read mode leads; an illustrative experiment adds an optional Experience surface.

## First viewport
Name, full academic biography, substantive research interests with descriptive references, and direct email/CV/publication links. The user rejected the previous low-density, exploration-first homepage. Recent news and representative publications follow immediately with compact spacing. Retain Fraunces / Source Sans 3 and the existing accent.

## Visitor path
Home establishes identity, research fit, recent work, and publication evidence without opening disclosures or visiting another page. Research themes and study pages provide optional depth. Publications independently supports search, topic/status filters, citation copying, direct anchors, and browser history. Writing connects existing posts without rewriting them.

## Signature interaction
An inline, deterministic toy population on the Chameleon study page illustrates the distinction between matching explicit persona attributes and preserving variation beyond those attributes. Two independent controls and three example states update actual plotted values. The figure explicitly labels synthetic data, describes its simplifying assumptions, and links to Chameleon. It is neither a live LLM nor a reproduction of the paper's metrics.
