# Research discovery and interactive explanation

## Scope
Extend the established academic visual identity across Home, Research, Publications, Writing, and three research detail pages. The user approved this architecture and selected interactive experiments as the personal signature. Read mode leads; an illustrative experiment adds an optional Experience surface.

## First viewport
Name and identity, a concise research statement, and the beginning of question-led discovery. Retain Fraunces / Source Sans 3, the existing accent, and generous reading space. Avoid decorative project cards and additional branding motifs.

## Visitor path
Home question → Research theme → study page → paper, code, actual demonstration or related question. Publications independently supports search, topic/status filters, citation copying, direct anchors, and browser history. Writing connects existing posts without rewriting them.

## Signature interaction
An inline, deterministic toy population illustrates the distinction between matching explicit persona attributes and preserving variation beyond those attributes. Two independent controls and three example states update actual plotted values. The figure explicitly labels synthetic data, describes its simplifying assumptions, and links to Chameleon. It is neither a live LLM nor a reproduction of the paper's metrics.

## Behavior and evidence
Static content remains readable without JavaScript. Filters and experiment settings are shareable via URL parameters. Native keyboard controls, visible focus, live feedback, full author names, mobile reflow, and light/dark contrast are required. Existing URLs and publication anchors remain valid. Real study findings and contributions come from canonical academic data; future directions are labeled.
