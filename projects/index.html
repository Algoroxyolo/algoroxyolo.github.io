The Chameleon's Limit

Validity of simulated populations

Do distinct persona profiles produce behaviorally diverse populations?

High persona fidelity can coexist with a homogeneous simulated population: convincing individuals do not guarantee population diversity.

We evaluate 10 language models and 1,144 personas across personality responses, moral judgments, and self-introductions.

Sentipolis

Emotion and collective dynamics

How do persistent emotions shape agent interactions over time?

Persistent emotion–memory coupling improves emotional continuity in the evaluated simulations; gains in believability depend on the model.

A 25-agent social simulation couples continuous emotional state with memory; component ablations, human judgments, and network diagnostics examine its effects.

My role: Co-first author.

TartanMaroon

Multi-agent collaboration in education

When does academic advising need coordination among specialized agents?

Iterative proposal–critique negotiation improves constrained degree planning, with greater benefits on complex tasks than on simple factual queries.

TartanBench evaluates advising across 220 queries and five complexity tiers; an interface makes agent coordination visible to users.

My role: Co-author; research mentor to Peidi Dong at Carnegie Mellon University Qatar.
